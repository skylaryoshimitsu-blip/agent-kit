description: Work alongside another developer on an assigned scope
argument-hint: <assigned scope>
---

Pair on: $ARGUMENTS

Follow the agent-kit rules, plus Section 8 (client work). Steps:

1. **Read their conventions first** (repo CLAUDE.md, CONTRIBUTING, lint/format config, recent PRs). Their conventions override the kit defaults.
2. **Confirm scope** in one message: exactly which files/areas are in scope. Anything outside is off-limits.
3. **Plan → approval** as usual (#approvals).
4. **Small PRs** against their default branch. Never push to protected branches. No refactors, renames, or formatting outside scope.
5. On a teammate's code: leave review comments instead of rewriting it.
6. Verify, post proof, request review from their team, then `/report`.
